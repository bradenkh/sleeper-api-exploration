# Trade targets & manager playbook

Per-manager notes for working trades. These are friends, so prodding is fine —
but see the timing note at the bottom, because waking the league up has a cost.

Ranks are Sleeper `search_rank`: a **global** ranking across all positions, with
ties (two players share rank 1). Lower is better. Best QB sits ~3, best WR ~3,
best TE ~22, kickers ~134+. It lags real-world news — directional, not fact.

**Need: wide receiver.** Smith-Njigba (6) and London (19) are real. Then a cliff

Fair value both ways, and it converts surplus at a deep position into a starter
at a thin one.

## Timing

**Don't send offers in Week 1.** Nothing has happened; nobody feels a need, and
an unprompted opening-week offer reads as random.

Wait for the weakness to bite:

- TuR7L3z gets a bad week or an injury from Gibbs/Barkley → he *feels* the thin
  RB room. He's active, so he's the exception worth approaching early.
- The six-WR managers watch good receivers rot on the bench → redundancy becomes
  self-evident without me arguing it.
- **Weeks 5–8 is the sweet spot.** Enough has happened to argue from, and the
  Week 11 deadline isn't yet forcing anything.

**Send offers with a message, not silently.** A trade notification in an app
nobody opens does nothing. A text — "sent you something, look at your RB depth"
— is what actually gets it seen.

---

## The cost of prodding

The single biggest edge this season is that **four of five managers have not
opened the app since the autodraft**. They're leaving startable free agents on
the wire and will start players on bye.

A group-chat nudge like "everyone check your lineups" wakes the whole league and
costs more than any single trade gains.

**So prod narrowly:** one person, one specific trade, without advertising that
the league is asleep.

---

## The line

Normal self-interested bargaining is the game. Leaning on a friend to accept
something plainly bad for them is collusion, against league rules, and would
sour a six-person friend league fast.

No shading is needed here anyway: RB-surplus-for-WR-surplus is a genuine win-win
on both sides, and the pitch is stronger *because* it's true.
